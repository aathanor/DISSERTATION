---
title: "Russell — found the mechanism, then restricted it"
kind: "Position in the debate"
related: [kripke, pru, line2, xo, local-rigidity]
---

Russell saw that demonstratives are the only natural-language terms that "just name an object without describing it." Everything else is a disguised description. This is the first identification of *local rigidity*: the special referential certainty of demonstrative use under acquaintance.

His retreat: restricting the mechanism to immediate sense-data, where identity is "exempt from all imaginable doubt." The Cartesian demand for infallibility forced him to treat the mechanism as a limiting case rather than as the cognitive primitive of reference.

The dissertation places Russell as positive, not as a foil. The PRU account aims to generalize what Russell identified by dropping the demand for infallibility.

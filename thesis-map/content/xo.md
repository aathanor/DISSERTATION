---
title: "$x^o$ — object-mode"
kind: "PRU notation"
related: [xo-xc, xc, R, local-rigidity]
---

$x^o$ marks a word coordinating with an encountered particular — "this $x$ here." It carries no descriptive content; the perceptual tracking itself constitutes the reference.

$x^o$ is the cognitive realization of primitive rigidity. It has no descriptive structure to interact with modal operators, so the demonstrative is scope-ambiguity-free. This is what names inherit through baptism.

Note: $x^o$ is still *language*. The experiential pole is $E\{X\}$. The link between them is $\mathcal{R}$, which is foundational developmentally, not a continuous requirement for competent use.

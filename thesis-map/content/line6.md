Baptism is the cognitive precondition for naming itself: a moment of demonstrative anchoring that gets lifted into a durable name-concept. Symbolically, $\mathcal{R}(h^o, N^c)$ exploits a demonstrative binding to inaugurate a durable name-concept.

Baptism does not *create* rigidity. It *lifts* primitive rigidity into $N^c$. The locus of rigidity shifts; its nature does not. Names inherit their scope-ambiguity-free character from the demonstrative act at their origin.

This is the sense in which names are *portable demonstratives*: devices engineered to export $x^o$-mode tracking across absence through social practice.

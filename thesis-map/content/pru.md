PRU offers a candidate for what Russell identified and what Kripke's apparatus presupposes: the cognitive operation underlying rigid tracking. Two moves do the work:

- **$x^o$ is the cognitive primitive Russell pointed to**, but it is fallible and dynamic, not infallible and punctual. Rigidity does not require infallibility — Kripke himself allows that we can be wrong about a bearer while still referring rigidly. What PRU needs is stable tracking, not Cartesian certainty.
- **Baptism is the lifting operation** that makes $x^o$ tracking portable: $\mathcal{R}(h^o, N^c)$. Names are devices engineered to export local tracking into the space of absence.

PRU does not revise Kripkean model theory. It supplies its cognitive preconditions.

**Objective.** The constructive proposal. Three-step dialectic: Russell saw the mechanism → Kripke described its semantic output → PRU explains how the mechanism scales.

## 5.1 The PRU Framework (Brief)

Pattern-Constellations as unified attractor states integrating sensory input and motor-affective response as one event. Pattern-Recognition Unity: recognition and reaction are inseparable aspects of a single event, not two stages. The grounding operator $\mathcal{R}(\{X\}, \langle x \rangle)$ connects the experiential dimension to the linguistic one. Only what the argument needs is introduced; the framework's full development belongs elsewhere.

## 5.2 Local Rigidity: $x^o$ as the Cognitive Primitive

$x^o$ is the PRU implementation of Russell's acquaintance — generalized, fallible, dynamic. $E(\{X\})$ is the experiential pole: an attractor basin shaped by prior encounters that the word $x^o$ coordinates with in the moment of use. The disanalogy with Russell is critical. Russell required Cartesian certainty: a demonstrative referent had to be "exempt from all imaginable doubt." PRU's grounding is fallible — the attractor can misfire, can be resculpted by later encounters — and dynamic, not punctual.

Rigidity does not require infallibility. Kripke himself allows that we can be wrong about a bearer's properties while still referring rigidly to it. PRU formalizes the same insight at the cognitive level: stable tracking is enough; certainty is not needed.

## 5.3 The Problem of Absence

Without names, rigid tracking dissolves when the bearer leaves. The demonstrative $x^o$ is bound to an encountered instance; once the encounter ends, the binding lapses. The descriptive fallback ("the one who did such-and-such") fails modally for the reasons Kripke gives — descriptions cannot preserve rigid tracking across counterfactual variation.

Names are *portable demonstratives*: devices engineered to export local tracking into the space of absence. They preserve what the encounter achieved, in a form that survives the end of the encounter and the entry of other speakers.

## 5.4 Baptism as Lifting

$\mathcal{R}(h^o, N^c)$: a baptismal act exploits an existing demonstrative binding ($h^o$ rigidly coordinated with a particular instance of $\{H\}$) and inaugurates a new name-concept $N^c$ whose standing function is to track that same individual.

This is not a metaphysical operation. It is a change in linguistic practice. Before baptism, the phonological form has no established role. Baptism does two things simultaneously: it exploits an existing demonstrative binding, and it inaugurates a linguistic pattern $\langle N \rangle$ whose function is to preserve that binding beyond the baptismal episode.

What shifts is the locus of rigidity, not its nature. Baptism does not *create* rigidity; it *lifts* the primitive rigidity of $x^o$ into $N^c$. Names inherit the scope-ambiguity-free character of the demonstrative act at their origin.

## 5.5 Causal Chains as Testimonial Networks

After baptism, later speakers rarely have direct experiential contact with the bearer. Their access runs through two kinds of structure:

- **Testimonial patterns $\tau(N)$:** statements, texts, narratives produced by people who knew the bearer or learned of the bearer from those who did. The chain of teaching, citation, and correction.
- **Surrogate constellations $\sigma(N)$:** manuscripts, portraits, inscriptions, recordings — objects that causally trace back to the original bearer and offer experiential anchoring even in the bearer's absence.

These implement Kripke's causal chains of communication at the cognitive level. The linguistic pattern $\langle N \rangle$ is sustained by the community's treating it as a tracking device, with norms of correction that catch deviations.

## 5.6 PRU as Metasemantics

PRU does not revise Kripkean model theory. In a standard Kripke model $\langle W, R, D, I \rangle$, a name $N$ receives a rigid interpretation: $I(N)$ picks out the same individual across all worlds where that individual exists. PRU does not alter this constraint. It supplies the cognitive preconditions: how speakers come to inhabit such a model, what makes the interpretation function determinate, what the practice of rigid tracking consists in.

The relationship is parallel to the relationship between formal logic and the cognitive practices that make logical reasoning possible. The model is not in competition with the practice; the practice is what the model formalizes.

The necessary *a posteriori* is treated here as a Kripkean result the account explains. "Hesperus is Phosphorus" is necessary at $x^o$ (one demonstrative pick, one individual) and *a posteriori* at $x^c$ (two intensional routes whose convergence is empirical). The full machinery — $\mathcal{R}$, the two modes, baptism, testimonial chains — is in place by the time this is stated.

$x^c$ marks a word operating in linguistic space — "the $x$," "$x$s are F," "$x$ resembles $y$." It navigates relations within $\langle x \rangle$.

$x^c$ is intensional through and through. It cannot, by itself, hold a particular: at $x^c$, reference passes through descriptions, kinds, properties.

Humans spend most of their time in $\langle x \rangle$ space, talking about objects they never encounter. The foundational grounding to $E\{X\}$ does not need to be continuously refreshed for competent use.

The diagnostic for LLMs (parking lot): LLMs operate exclusively in $x^c$ mode, without the foundational developmental phase that enables $\mathcal{R}$. Their hallucinations are a structural symptom, not a noise term to be engineered away.

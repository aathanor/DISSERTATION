Three things Kripke's apparatus does not deliver, openly acknowledged in the post-Kripkean literature (LaPorte's "promissory note" formulation):

- What cognitive operation a speaker performs when they rigidly track an individual across counterfactual variation.
- What baptism is, at the cognitive level — what changes in a speaker's relation to a bearer when a name is conferred.
- How a causal chain of communication is psychologically realized — what makes a transmission preserve reference rather than drift.

Existing implementations (Evans, Recanati, Devitt) point at the right desideratum but pull the explanation back into the representational/propositional register. None explains the primitive.

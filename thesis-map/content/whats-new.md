**Claimed as new:**

- The diagnostic reading of Kripke's formula: the three-way equivocation under "object" between mathematical, encountered, and named individuals.
- The dialectical placement of Russell as positive — the thinker who found the mechanism and was held back by the Cartesian setting (interpretive).
- The $x^o$ / $x^c$ disambiguation of identity, rigidity, and the necessary a posteriori.
- Local rigidity as the primitive form of reference; baptism as lifting (not creating) rigidity.
- The Wittgenstein 5.5303 connection to Kripke's identity argument, surprisingly underdeveloped in the literature.
- The grouping of Evans / Recanati / Devitt under a shared representationalist limitation (interpretive).

**Inherited (not claimed as new):**

- Kripke's modal/epistemic/semantic arguments for rigidity and the rigidity definition.
- The PRU framework itself (developed independently in the framework paper).
- Wittgenstein 5.5303 as a logical observation about identity.
- Russell's observation about demonstratives as the only "real names."
- Kaplan's *dthat*; Schwarz's circularity diagnosis (related but upstream).

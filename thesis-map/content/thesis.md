## The observation

Language by itself is intensional through and through: $\langle x \rangle$ relates words to words, meanings to meanings, inferential roles to inferential roles. Yet language contains devices — demonstratives, indexicals, proper names — whose function is to open onto something extensional. These devices don't *perform* the connection by themselves. *This* without a gesture and an object is empty. *John* without a baptismal history is a sound. Language provides the slot; the world fills it.

The same observation surfaces across philosophy of language under different names: Russell's distinction between knowledge by acquaintance and knowledge by description; Kripke's separation of rigid designation from descriptive content; the modal failure of definite descriptions; baptism as the device that makes a particular encounter portable across time and speakers. The dissertation takes the Kripke case as its target.

## The thesis

**Rigid designation is the social stabilization of $x^o$-mode tracking across absence.**

Three corollaries:

- The necessity of identity is a consequence of $x^o$-mode designation, not a property of identity itself.
- The necessary *a posteriori* arises because the same identity statement is necessary at the $x^o$ level and discoverable at the $x^c$ level.
- PRU supplies the cognitive mechanism that Kripke's formal apparatus stipulates without explaining.

The position is metasemantic, not revisionist: it does not propose changes to Kripke's model theory; it supplies its cognitive preconditions.

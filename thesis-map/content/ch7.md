**The argument reconstructed.** Wittgenstein showed identity is always between designators. Kripke's formula captures this at the $x^o$ level (trivially). Russell identified the mechanism (acquaintance = $x^o$) and retreated. Kripke described the semantic output. PRU closes the gap.

**Open questions for further work:**

- Fictional names as unsaturated patterns ($Sherlock^c$ with no baptismal $\mathcal{R}$).
- Natural kind terms: the $x^o$ / $x^c$ disambiguation as a research program.
- Formal development of $\mathcal{R}$ (parked: as projective operator in PRU framework paper).
- The LLM diagnostic: $x^c$ without $x^o$.

1. **Identity is between designators, not objects.** Wittgenstein 5.5303; Russell's confirmation. The formula is trivially valid because variables are $x^o$-type extensional pointers rigid by construction. (Ch. 2)

2. **Kripke's gloss performs an unacknowledged three-way shift.** "Object" bridges variables → instances → names. The Benjamin Franklin passage shows the slide. (Ch. 2)

3. **The $x^o$ / $x^c$ distinction disambiguates identity, rigidity, and the necessary a posteriori.** (Ch. 4)

4. **$x^o$ is the cognitive primitive.** Russell saw it as acquaintance, restricted it to sense-data. PRU drops the infallibility demand. (Ch. 4, 5)

5. **Names are portable demonstratives.** $\mathcal{R}(h^o, N^c)$ lifts primitive rigidity into $N^c$. Baptism is the lifting event, not the source of rigidity. (Ch. 5)

6. **Causal chains are testimonial networks.** $\tau(N)$ and $\sigma(N)$ implement reference transmission at the cognitive level. (Ch. 5)

7. **PRU is metasemantics for Kripkean rigidity.** It supplies the cognitive preconditions of the model theory; it does not revise it. (Ch. 5)

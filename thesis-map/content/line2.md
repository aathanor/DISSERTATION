Russell identified that demonstratives are the only "real proper names" — words that "just name an object without describing it." He recognized referential certainty under acquaintance, then restricted it to immediate sense data.

PRU's $x^o$ is the cognitive implementation of acquaintance, with two critical differences: PRU's grounding is fallible, not infallible (the attractor basin can misfire and can be resculpted by later encounters); and it is dynamic, not punctual.

These differences are not weaknesses. Rigidity does not require infallibility. The fallibility makes PRU more plausible as a cognitive account, not less.

(Honest framing note: this is dialectical work — Russell as the thinker who found and retreated — rather than independent evidence for local rigidity. The historical placement is the dissertation's interpretive move; the content is inherited.)

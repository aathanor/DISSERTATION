The necessary a posteriori is structurally explained by the $x^o / x^c$ distinction. "Hesperus is Phosphorus" is:

- **Necessary at $x^o$:** one individual, one demonstrative pick — the identity is constitutive.
- **A posteriori at $x^c$:** two intensional routes, two observational practices, two inferential networks — convergence is empirical.

The same identity statement is read at two levels. What looks paradoxical disappears once the two modes are kept apart.

Placement in the dissertation: Chapter 5, late in the chapter (§5.6 or a closing subsection), as a Kripkean result the framework should account for — handled after the full machinery ($\mathcal{R}$, the two modes, baptism) is in place.

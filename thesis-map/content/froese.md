Comment paper responding to Froese (2026), DOI: 10.1007/s11097-025-10132-0, on enactivism and LLMs.

Froese's move: a mea culpa on behalf of enactivism. Embodied/enactive theorists maintained "categorical exclusionism" about AI; LLMs have demonstrated competence at a level that makes blanket exclusion untenable. He proposes accepting LLMs as a novel non-biological form of sense-maker with "technologically-mediated embodiment" — corpora carrying fossil traces of embodied coupling.

**The PRU position:** LLMs operate exclusively in $x^c$ mode. They lack the foundational developmental phase that enables $\mathcal{R}$. Their language use is ungrounded in Froese's relevant sense. Hallucination is a structural symptom of $x^c$-only operation, not a noise term.

This *sharpens* rather than softens the contrast with Froese's "fossil traces" position. The dilemma he is forced into (either LLMs sense-make, or linguistic competence doesn't require sense-making) dissolves once $x^c$ competence is distinguished from $\mathcal{R}$-grounded sense-making. Competence and grounding were never the same thing.
